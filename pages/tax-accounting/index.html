What most non-resident owners don't realize about Spanish tax

Spanish tax for non-residents isn't complicated because the rules are unclear. It's complicated because almost nobody explains it before it becomes a problem.

01

Believing that paying IBI (the local council tax) covers everything owed to the Spanish tax authorities — it doesn't; it's a separate, local tax.

02

Not knowing that an empty, unrented property still generates an annual tax bill, simply for being owned by a non-resident.

03

Discovering that the tax rate and the deductions allowed are genuinely different depending on whether you live in the EU/EEA or not.

04

Missing the filing deadline and only finding out when a penalty notice arrives, with interest already added.

05

Assuming the 3% withheld on a sale automatically settles everything — it's only a payment on account, not the final tax bill.

None of this needs to catch you out when someone is tracking your deadlines every year, not just the year you asked for help.
