What we do best.

The three areas that bring most of our international clients to us. Each one handled directly by Mar or Vicente: not delegated, not outsourced.

01

Buying and Selling Properties in Spain

Whether you are buying or selling, we secure the transaction from start to finish. For sellers, we manage tax withholding reviews, capital gains calculations, tax liquidations, and claim tax refunds (like the 3% non-resident withholding) when applicable. For buyers, we perform full due diligence and structure the deal safely.

02

Spanish Inheritance

Dealing with an inheritance in Spain from abroad is one of the most stressful things a family can face. The deadlines are strict, the taxes are significant, and the paperwork is entirely in Spanish. Mar handles every step personally — so your family doesn't have to navigate the Spanish system alone during an already difficult time. We work in English and German throughout.

03

Spanish Wills

A Spanish will is one of the most important things you can do for your family before you need it. Without one, your heirs face months of extra paperwork, higher costs and avoidable delays. We draft it, sign it before a Spanish notary and register it correctly. We'll tell you honestly whether you need one here.
