### Buying property in Spain

Most people who get into trouble with a Spanish property purchase didn't do anything wrong. They just didn't know what to look for and neither did whoever was supposed to be advising them.

Before you sign anything, we review the full legal and urban status of the property. That means:

- Extensions built without planning permission
- Debts or charges registered against the property that the seller hasn't mentioned
- Registry entries that affect ownership or would complicate a future sale
- Constructions that appear on site but don't exist on paper — a problem that surfaces at the worst possible moment

Once you're satisfied, we handle your **NIE, the arras contract, notary representation, tax settlement and utility transfers.** You don't need to be in Spain for any of it if you'd rather not be.

### Selling property in Spain

**Selling as a non-resident in Spain** comes with tax obligations that catch most people off guard: not because they're hidden, but because nobody ever explained them:

- The 3% the buyer retains at the time of sale and pays directly to the tax authority
- Capital gains tax on the profit from the sale
- Plusvalía municipal, calculated on the increase in land value

Each of these has rules, deadlines and, in some cases, the right to a refund, but only if the paperwork is done correctly and on time. **We handle all of it.** If you can't be in Spain, you don't need to be.

### Spanish inheritance

When someone passes away with **property in Spain**, their heirs usually find themselves dealing with an unfamiliar legal system, in a language they don't speak, with a six-month deadline they didn't know about.

We guide heirs through the entire process:

We've handled hundreds of these files. The process doesn't have to be as difficult as it looks from the outside.

### Spanish will

If you own property in Spain and you don't have a **Spanish will**, your heirs will eventually deal with the consequences. A will made in your home country doesn't automatically apply to Spanish assets in the most straightforward way and the extra cost and delay it creates falls on them, not you.

Making a Spanish will takes very little time:

- One conversation to understand what you want
- A draft reviewed together, in plain language
- Signature at the notary
- Registration in the Spanish Registry of Last Wills

**It's one of the simplest things you can do for the people you'll leave behind.**

## Everything else that comes with owning property in Spain

Property transactions and inheritance are the core of what we do, but owning property in Spain involves a long tail of administrative and legal obligations. Our clients don't deal with five different offices for five different things. We cover it all.

### NIE — Foreigner Identification Number

You need one before you can do almost anything in Spain. We manage the application and follow-up so you don't have to work through the bureaucracy yourself.

### Power of attorney

Can't be in Spain for a purchase, sale or inheritance procedure? We prepare the legal authority that lets us act on your behalf, so you don't have to travel for every step.

### Residency and TIE

If you're planning to live in Spain full-time, or need to formalise your status, we handle the application and all supporting documentation. For British nationals, we also advise on the specific requirements under the post-Brexit Withdrawal Agreement.

### Tax filing for non-residents

If you own property in Spain and don't live here, you have a tax obligation — even if the property sits empty. We prepare and file the returns on your behalf and keep you compliant.

### Property taxes and utility transfers

When a property changes hands, everything registered in the seller's name needs to move to yours: IBI, community fees, rubbish collection, electricity, water. We handle all of it so nothing is forgotten.

### Tourist rental licence

Renting to tourists requires a specific licence and compliance with regional regulations. We manage the entire application process.

### Donations

Transferring assets to family members during your lifetime can be a more tax-efficient way to pass on your estate than waiting. The Valencian Community and Murcia offer significant reductions on donations between parents and children. We advise on the implications and handle the legal process from start to finish.

### Mortgages

Before you sign any financing agreement, we review the terms and make sure the legal side is in order.

### Company formation

Some investors prefer to hold property through a Spanish company rather than in their own name. We handle incorporation and the ongoing legal obligations that come with it.

## Frequently asked questions about legal services in Spain for foreigners

### Do I need a lawyer to buy property in Spain as a foreigner?

It's not a legal requirement. But a lawyer who is working for you — not for the seller, not for the agent — is the only person at that table whose job it is to find the problems. Without one, you're trusting that everything is fine without anyone actually checking. Most of the time it is. When it isn't, it's rarely a small problem.

### Can I handle everything from abroad?

Yes. Most of our clients never set foot in a Spanish office. With a power of attorney we can act on your behalf for purchases, sales, inheritance procedures, tax filings and most administrative matters. You're kept informed at every step: you just don't have to travel for it.

### What languages do you work in?

English, Spanish and German. Every consultation, document and piece of correspondence can be handled in English, or whichever of these languages you prefer. You should always understand exactly what you're signing and why.

### How quickly do you respond?

Within 24 hours, Monday to Friday. If your situation is urgent, say so when you write and we'll treat it accordingly.

### I already have a will at home. Do I really need a Spanish one?

In most cases, yes. A foreign will doesn't automatically apply to Spanish assets in the simplest way. Without a Spanish will, your heirs may face delays, extra costs and procedures that could have been avoided entirely. The process takes one meeting and a signature. It's one of the most straightforward things we do, and one of the most useful.

### What is the 3% withholding when I sell as a non-resident?

When a non-resident sells property in Spain, the buyer is legally required to hold back 3% of the sale price and pay it to the tax authority directly. It's an advance payment on capital gains tax. If your actual liability is less than 3% — or if you made a loss — you're entitled to a refund. But you have to claim it, and within the deadline. We handle the filing and the recovery.

### How long does a Spanish inheritance take?

The legal deadline for settling inheritance tax is six months from the date of death, extendable by another six months if requested in time. The full process — from gathering documents to receiving the registered title — typically takes between three and twelve months. Complexity varies considerably. The sooner you start, the more options you have.
