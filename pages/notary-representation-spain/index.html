Notary representation in Spain: you don't have to be there to sign.

Buying a property, signing a mortgage, accepting an inheritance, or granting a power of attorney all go through the same step: a notary. A Spanish notary isn't just someone who watches you sign. They're a public official who checks the legality of what you're signing, explains it to you, and gives the document full legal force — a different role from the notary you might know back home, and the difference matters.

At Sol-4 Gestión, we represent you at the notary so you don't have to fly to Spain for a 20-minute signing. With the right power of attorney in place, we sign on your behalf, start to finish.

Assuming a Spanish notary works like the one back home — it doesn't. They verify the whole transaction, not just your signature.
