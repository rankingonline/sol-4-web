25 years of the same focus,
on the same stretch of coast.

Sol-4 Gestión was founded in 2001 on the Costa Blanca, in Alicante province, by A. Vicente Sáez and Mar Bascuñana. From the beginning, the firm focused on something specific: helping foreign buyers and property owners navigate the Spanish legal and tax system correctly, completely and in a language they understood.

Over 25 years, that focus has remained the same. What has grown is the depth of the team, the range of services and the number of clients: more than 2,500 operations handled, across property purchases, sales, inheritances, wills and the full range of legal and tax matters that come with owning property in Spain.

We have been in practically the same location for 25 years. We started at Calle Aneto, 2 and today we are right next door, at Calle Aneto, 1, in Benijófar. For clients who visit in person, the team is here. For clients who manage everything remotely — which is most of them — we are reachable by phone, email and WhatsApp, and we respond within 24 hours.

Not values on a wall.
Things our clients mention when they refer us.

Several things define how Sol-4 Gestión operates. They are not aspirational statements — they are what our clients say about us when they recommend us to someone else.

We respond, every time

The most common complaint foreign clients have about legal advisors in Spain is silence. A question asked, a message sent, and then nothing for days. We don't work that way. Every enquiry gets a response within 24 hours, Monday to Friday. If we don't have the answer yet, we say so — and we say when we will.

We speak your language

Another common complaint about advisors in Spain is language: documents in legal Spanish that no one explains to you. Not with us. We work in Spanish, English and German, and we make sure you understand every document before you sign.

We go further than the paperwork

Anyone can review the documents the seller provides. We review the property itself, checking for undeclared extensions, planning irregularities and physical issues that don't appear in the registry but matter enormously after the purchase. It's one of the things that sets us apart.

We stay until it's done

Our job doesn't end at the notary. It ends when every utility is in your name, every tax is filed, every account is closed or transferred and you have nothing left outstanding in Spain. That's what complete legal service means to us.
