NIE in Spain: we apply for it, so you don't have to.

Almost every legal or financial step in Spain — buying a property, opening a bank account, settling an inheritance, paying a tax bill — starts with the same three letters: NIE. It's not residency, and it's not a work permit. It's simply the number Spain uses to identify you, and without it, nothing else can move forward.

At Sol-4 Gestión, we apply for your NIE as part of the process you actually need it for — or on its own if that's all you need. Most of our clients never set foot in a Spanish office to get it: we do it by power of attorney.

What most foreign applicants don't realize about the NIE

Spain's paperwork rarely fails at the big moments. It fails at the small ones nobody warned you about.

01

Thinking an NIE means you're a resident — it doesn't, and the two get confused constantly. Residency is a completely separate process.

02

Booking an appointment weeks out, only to find the office fully booked when the date arrives — or the appointment system offline.

03

Stating the wrong reason for the application — "residency" instead of "property purchase," for example — and getting rejected on a technicality.

04

Not knowing that a power of attorney lets someone else apply and collect it for you — so a trip to Spain isn't needed at all.

05

Finding out you need an NIE in the middle of a property purchase or an inheritance, when there's suddenly no time to spare.

None of these happen when you have someone who does this regularly handling it for you.

How the process works: step by step

01

Initial consultation

We ask why you need your NIE — a purchase, an inheritance, a bank account, a tax matter — because the reason determines the fastest route and the documents required.

02

Choosing the right route

In Spain, through a consulate, or by power of attorney. We confirm which one fits your situation and timeline before moving forward.

03

Power of attorney

Signed remotely or at a notary near you, this lets us apply, pay the fee, and collect your NIE on your behalf, start to finish — no travel to Spain required.

04

Filing the application

We complete and file form EX-15, submit your passport copy and the stated justification, and pay the official fee (Modelo 790-012) on your behalf.

05

Collection and certified copy

Once your NIE is assigned, we collect it and send you a certified copy — ready for your notary, your bank, or your tax filing.

06

Linking it to your process

If your NIE was for a property purchase or an inheritance, we carry straight on with that process — your NIE doesn't sit idle once it's issued.
