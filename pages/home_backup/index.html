Our Philosophy

You're not hiring a law firm.
You're gaining two partners who know your file personally.

Most people who come to us have been told Spain is complicated. They're right — it is. What they need isn't a list of services. They need someone who explains what's happening, flags problems before they become expensive, and stays involved from the first email to the last tax return.

That's what Mar and Vicente have been doing since 2001. We don't just check the documents. We check the property itself on site, before you sign anything. We don't just file the tax return. We model the tax impact before you commit. And we don't disappear once the deal is closed — your annual taxes, your next purchase, your will: we're still here for all of it.

02

Spanish Inheritance

Dealing with an inheritance in Spain from abroad is one of the most stressful things a family can face. The deadlines are strict, the taxes are significant, and the paperwork is entirely in Spanish. Mar handles every step personally — so your family doesn't have to navigate the Spanish system alone during an already difficult time. We work in English and German throughout.
03

Spanish Wills

A Spanish will is one of the most important things you can do for your family before you need it. Without one, your heirs face months of extra paperwork, higher costs and avoidable delays. We draft it, sign it before a Spanish notary and register it correctly. We'll tell you honestly whether you need one here.
